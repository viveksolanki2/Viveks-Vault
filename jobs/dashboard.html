How this runs

  1. 01At 9:00 AM PT the scanner reads LinkedIn's public listings, screens them against your criteria, and drops new matches here.
  2. 02Each new posting gets a one-page resume tailored to its description, built only from your master profile. Banned phrasing is blocked at the source.
  3. 03Everything commits to the repo and you get a push notification and an email.
  4. 04You hit Apply, attach the resume that was written for that role, and submit.

Why step 4 is yours. Submitting through Easy Apply needs your logged-in LinkedIn session. Handing a scheduled cloud job your session cookie means storing a live credential, and automated applying is the specific thing LinkedIn's User Agreement bans and screens for. The penalty is account restriction. Your LinkedIn is where Rahul, Tejas, and Sam reach you, so it is worth more than the clicks it would save.

Easy Apply also asks per-employer screening questions. Salary expectations and "years with SQL" cannot be unanswered once submitted, and guessing on those is exactly the accuracy problem you asked me to protect against.

If you want the clicking automated anyway, that runs on your machine through the job-apply plugin, in your own browser, with you watching. Say the word and I will set it up.
