 At 7 Leaves Cafe, we're more than a coffee and tea company. We are a people-first organization passionate about creating memorable experiences for our guests and meaningful careers for our team members. As we continue to grow, we're looking for a Corporate Staff Accountant who is excited to make an impact in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

In this role, you'll be a key member of our Finance & Accounting team, leading company-wide payroll and benefits administration while supporting month-end close, financial reporting, reconciliations, and process improvements.

 What You'll Do

 Payroll & Benefits

 - Own the full-cycle payroll process for corporate and multi-location cafe employees.
- Review and validate employee timecards, resolve payroll discrepancies, and ensure accurate payroll processing.
- Administer employee benefits, including medical, dental, vision, 401(k), FSA/HSA, and leave-related deductions.
- Coordinate open enrollment, qualifying life events, and benefits changes.
- Ensure compliance with California payroll laws, payroll tax requirements, and employee recordkeeping.
- Support payroll tax filings and maintain accurate payroll records.

 Accounting & Financial Operations

 - Prepare journal entries, accruals, reconciliations, and month-end close activities.
- Reconcile bank accounts, payroll liabilities, and corporate credit cards.
- Support financial reporting, variance analysis, and audit preparation.
- Maintain accurate records across multiple legal entities using Sage Intacct.
- Help identify opportunities to improve accounting processes and strengthen internal controls.

 What We're Looking For - Required Qualifications

 - 2+ years of accounting and payroll experience, preferably supporting multiple locations.
- Experience processing California payroll and understanding California wage and hour requirements.
- Knowledge of GAAP and general accounting principles.
- Experience with accounting and payroll systems such as Sage Intacct, Paylocity, or similar ERP/payroll platforms a Huge Plus!!
- Advanced Microsoft Excel skills (Pivot Tables, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, etc.).
- Exceptional organizational skills with strong attention to detail and accuracy.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities while meeting deadlines.

 Preferred Qualifications

 - Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
- Experience in the restaurant, retail, hospitality, or food & beverage industry.
- Experience administering employee benefits and supporting multi-entity accounting environments.
