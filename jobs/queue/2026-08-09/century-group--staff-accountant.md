 Century Group is partnering with a client that is seeking a Staff Accountant to join their team. Exact compensation may vary based on skills, experience, and location. Expected starting base salary of $60,000 to $80,000 per year.

 Job Responsibilities

 - Manage accounts payable activities by reviewing invoices processing payments and maintaining accurate vendor records. 
- Monitor cash activity and reconcile bank accounts to support financial accuracy and short term cash planning. 
- Support month end close processes through preparation of journal entries account reconciliations and financial reviews. 
- Collaborate with departments across the organization to gather financial information and support reporting needs. 
- Assist with audits financial analyses and process improvement initiatives that enhance reporting efficiency. 

 Requirements

 - A Bachelor degree in accounting finance or a related field. 
- 3+ years of accounting experience within a professional business environment. 
- Demonstrate experience with enterprise accounting systems and financial reporting processes. 
- Possess advanced Excel skills and experience working with financial data and reporting tools. 
- Work effectively in a fast paced environment while managing multiple priorities and deadlines. 

 Qualifications

 - Apply strong analytical and problem solving skills to evaluate financial results and identify trends. 
- Maintain exceptional attention to detail and accuracy when handling accounting records and reconciliations. 
- Communicate effectively with management team members and cross functional stakeholders. 
- Support inventory accounting activities and operational reporting experience considered beneficial. 
- Demonstrate initiative professionalism and the ability to work independently while contributing to team goals.
