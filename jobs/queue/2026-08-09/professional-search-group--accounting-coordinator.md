 Bilingual Accounting Coordinator (Hindi/English) – Los Angeles, CA 
 

 We're hiring a Bilingual Accounting Coordinator to join a growing IT and infrastructure solutions company in Los Angeles. This is a great opportunity for someone who is currently fluent in Hindi and English and wants to grow into a broader accounting role supporting both AP and AR functions, including direct work with international clients based in India.
 

 What You'll Do 
 - Support accounts payable and accounts receivable functions across US and India based accounts
- Communicate directly with India based clients in Hindi as needed
- Process invoices, handle reconciliations, and maintain accurate account documentation in Excel
- Flag and resolve discrepancies proactively
- Partner with the internal accounting team on billing and account issues
 

 What We're Looking For 
 - Current, fluent Hindi speaker with strong English communication skills
- Solid Excel skills
- Comfortable learning new computer systems
- Dependable, sharp, and able to work independently once trained
- Available to work 100% onsite in Los Angeles
 

 Nice to Have 
 - Prior AP or AR experience
- Experience with invoice processing or three-way matching
- Background at a product or goods based company
- Experience with Microsoft Dynamics GP (Great Plains)
 

 This role offers hands-on training and real ownership from day one, with room to grow as the company expands its international accounts. If you're bilingual in Hindi and English and looking for a stable, onsite accounting role with growth potential, we'd love to hear from you.
