The Role

We have kicked off a search for a Sales Planning & Reporting Analyst to develop, implement, and administer Honest's sales analytics You will work with internal and external cross-functional partners to deliver on company goals and objectives. You'll be a key player within the Sales team, supporting Sales Operations, Sales Planning and Field Sales, while working with cross-functional partners in achieving its strategic and operational goals. You're the right person for the job if you're curious & optimistic, consumer-first, work independently, equally analytical and entrepreneurial, able to be scrappy, flexible and solutions-oriented in a fast-paced environment.

 What you'll do:

 - Trade Management System (Upclear Blue Planner) super user and report owner.
- Leads the team in usage of the tool including baseline input, forecast input, and assortment management
- Experience with Demand Planning systems that use Trade Management data.
- Delivers data driven insights and customized reporting to inform trade investment decisions.
- Uses PowerPoint, Excel, Metabase, Power BI and Upclear data to present actionable insights to the sales trade and planning team, to aid in achieving yearly goals.
- Supports the sales team and the organization with training and support on released updates in the trade management tool.
- Works with sales, IT and Upclear to resolve any identified issues, updates or process challenges. Identifies and presents process changes leveraging data analytics.
- Onboards and keeps the sales team updated on all reports they receive or use (Hubble, Meta base)
- Customer Item Setup support including, WERC's, Syndigo GDSN, IXONE, and Top 3 Customer portal updates as needed.
- Co-Lead as Project Analyst of the New Customer Launch & Setup process.
- Supports the sales team with sku discontinuation and transition communications
- Leads the organization of the sales team Box folder and Retail Spot light slack channel
- Backup to the Pricing & Trade Analyst (Critical daily duties only)

 You'll love this job if you're:

 - Purposeful: Detail oriented and can manage time appropriately. Ability to juggle multiple priorities at once successfully
- Authentic : Pride yourself on providing data accuracy through data integrity 
- Purposeful/Authentic : Possess strong verbal and written communication skills
- Curious : Motivated by fast-paced, rapidly changing environments
- Compassion : A self-starter who loves collaboration
- Compassion : Comfortable working independently as well as with cross-functional teams
- Curious : Interested in project management, data analysis, and systems

 What you'll need:

 - Bachelor's degree
- 3-5 years of analytical + business experience in a fast-paced environment
- Proficiency in MS Office (PowerPoint, Excel, Word) and Google Docs
- Experience with Metabase, Power BI, Upclear Blue Planner, Syndigo, JD Edwards or equivalent systems
- Strong analytical skills and a creative approach to problem solving.
- Excellent presentation, communication, and relationship management skills
