 Job Summary 
 The Accounting Analyst is primarily responsible for processing and maintaining fixed assets, prepaid accounts, and is responsible for executing a variety of financial processes including month-end and year-end closing, general ledger reviews, variance analysis, account reconciliations, and assisting with quarterly regulatory reporting.
 Duties And Responsibilities 
 - Responsible for monthly fixed assets and prepaid accounting
- Supports daily liquidity and funds management.
- Prepare general ledger certifications (i.e. correspondent bank accounts, asset/liability general leger accounts and bank control accounts) and applicable schedules.
- Assist in the month end close process and reporting, including variance analysis.
- Assist in preparing the quarterly Call Report and supporting schedules
- Supports preparation of various analytical reports and information
- Analyze financial information to identify discrepancies
- Research and resolve discrepancies timely
- Collaborates with different departments to achieve the various deliverables on financial reporting.
- Builds and maintains rapport with internal stakeholders and external agencies involved in the financial reporting process.
- Additional tasks and responsibilities as assigned
- Expected attendance in office – 3 x week
 Qualifications 
 - Minimum of 3 years of Accounting Experience
- Commercial banking accounting experience preferred
- Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance from an accredited university
- Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite, including Outlook, Excel, Word, and Power Point
- Minimum of intermediate experience in Excel
 Skills And Competencies 
 - Performing tasks with minimal supervision
- Proactive and self-starter
- Independent critical thinker
- Excellent written/verbal communication and presentation skills
- Excellent organizational and time management skills, with the ability to multi-task and manage shifting priorities and accomplish deliverables accurately and on time.
- Ability to write reports, business correspondence and detailed procedures.
- Ability to effectively present information and respond to questions from other team members, managers, clients, customers, and vendors.
 Technical Skills
 - Strives to continuously build knowledge and skills
- Working knowledge of accounting related software
