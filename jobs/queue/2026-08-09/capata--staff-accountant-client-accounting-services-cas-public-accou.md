 Minimum 2 years of full-time employment in a licensed CPA/public accounting firm performing client accounting services. Experience working only in a private company's accounting department does not satisfy this requirement. 
 

 We are hiring for a Staff Accountant for a CPA firm located in Newport Beach, CA. This role supports outsourced accounting, CFO advisory, and client accounting functions.
 This is ideal for a motivated accountant with 2–3 years of relevant experience who enjoys client-facing work, clear processes, and professional growth in a high-caliber environment.
 Compensation: $55,000 – $75,000
 

 Core Responsibilities 
 Client Accounting & Advisory Support 
 - Perform accurate and timely bookkeeping for multiple clients
- Prepare and review account reconciliations and organized workpapers
- Support month-end and year-end close processes
- Prepare accurate financial statements and management reports
- Assist with payroll processing and related filings (as applicable)
- Support sales tax filings and business license renewals when required
- Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and cash-flow analysis as needed
- Identify potential accounting issues and propose practical solutions
- Collaborate with senior accountants, controllers, or fractional CFOs on client work
 

 Judgment, Communication & Workflow 
 - Maintain clear, professional communication with clients and internal teams
- Manage multiple client engagements and deadlines
- Follow firm accounting policies, procedures, and documentation standards
- Escalate issues appropriately with thoughtful context and recommendations
- Seek feedback proactively and demonstrate continuous improvement
 

 What Success Looks Like 
 A strong candidate will:
 - Deliver accurate, timely accounting work with limited supervision
- Document work clearly so it is easy for reviewers to follow
- Communicate early when stuck and ask thoughtful questions
- Demonstrate accountability for mistakes and learning
- Show readiness to take on more complex clients over time
 

 Qualifications (Required) 
 - Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or related field
- 2–3 years of relevant experience in public accounting, outsourced accounting, or advisory services
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to manage multiple engagements concurrently
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook)
- Solid understanding of bookkeeping and accounting principles (GAAP)
- High attention to detail and accuracy
- Strong organizational skills and ability to prioritize work
- Ability to handle confidential information with discretion
 

 Education & Professional Development (Preferred) 
 - CPA eligibility or progress toward CPA is a plus (not required)
- Master’s in Accounting or related field is a plus
- Experience with QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, or similar platforms is helpful
 

 Work Model & Placement 
 - Hybrid role in Newport Beach, CA
- Overtime may be required during peak periods depending on the client
 

 Application Process

- Questions from candidates may be sent through LinkedIn
- Phone calls and external recruiter submissions will not be considered
