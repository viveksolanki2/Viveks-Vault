## Job description

 Join a stable and growing organization seeking a Staff Accountant to support general accounting operations, month-end close, financial reporting, and audit activities. This is an excellent opportunity for an accounting professional looking to develop their career in a collaborative, team-oriented environment. 

Responsibilities

 - Process accounts payable, accounts receivable, and employee expense reports
- Prepare journal entries, accruals, and account reconciliations
- Assist with month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close
- Reconcile balance sheet accounts and bank accounts
- Support financial reporting and audit requests
- Maintain accurate accounting records and documentation
- Partner with internal teams to resolve accounting issues and improve processes

 Qualifications

 - Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Finance required
- 1-3+ years of accounting experience
- Understanding of AP processes and accrual accounting
- Knowledge of GAAP and/or IFRS
- Strong Excel skills (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUPs)
- Detail-oriented with strong analytical and communication skills

 Preferred

 - Journal entry and general ledger experience
- Month-end close exposure
- SAP, Dynamics 365, or other ERP experience
- Public accounting experience is a plus

 Why Apply?

 - Hybrid schedule with flexibility
- Annual bonus and profit-sharing opportunities
- 401(k) match and strong benefits package
- Outstanding company culture and low employee turnover
- Excellent training, mentorship, and career growth opportunities
