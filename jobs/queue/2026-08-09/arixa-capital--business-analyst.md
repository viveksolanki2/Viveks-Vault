 Position Overview

 The Business Analyst will play a critical role in the company's operations department, assisting with technology implementation and processes in its primary database. This position requires a strong background in business technology and working with development teams, specifically within Salesforce environments.

 Summary of Primary Duties and Responsibilities

 The Business Analyst is an LA-based position on a hybrid schedule with at least two (2) days in-office. This position will report to the VP, Loan Operations - Servicing. While not an exhaustive list, the Business Analyst will be responsible for:

 - Work on technological implementations in Salesforce
- Create and run ad hoc reporting from Salesforce
- Lay out new fields and processes for Salesforce and work with the development team to test functionality
- Write procedures for maintaining data integrity in our various systems
- Create process documents outlining data procedures and new updates in Salesforce
- Assist with data cleanup projects
- Analyze existing processes for inefficiencies and redundancies, and propose improvements
- Train team members on Salesforce procedures
- Troubleshoot Salesforce performance issues and work with the development team to resolve errors

 Education and Experience

 - Bachelor's Degree in Business, Economics, Real Estate, or related field.
- 2-3 years of experience in business analysis and operations, with a proven track record of working with large datasets and performing complex analyses.
- 1-2 years of experience in the mortgage or real estate industry, with a solid understanding of mortgage products preferred.
- Proficiency in data analysis tools and software such as Excel, SQL, and data visualization platforms.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret complex data and provide actionable insights.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to present findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
- Excellent organizational and time management skills.
- Knowledge of the Agile Methodologies and Atlassian (Jira) platform.
- High attention to detail and accuracy.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, Outlook).
- Ability to multi-task and prioritize assigned tasks / projects with minimal assistance.
- Knowledge of system integrations and the ability to assess their impact on business processes and requirements.
- Expert troubleshooting skills to investigate resolve and escalate problems.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Salesforce certifications are a plus
